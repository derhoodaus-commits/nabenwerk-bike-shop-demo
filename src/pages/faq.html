FAQ

Questions we hear at the counter

Don’t see your question? Send it through our contact form. We reply within one business day.

Appointments and process

How long does a repair take?

Small jobs like a tube change usually take less than an hour. Tune-ups are normally done the same day if you drop the bike off in the morning.

Do I need an appointment?

For tune-ups and bigger repairs, yes. For a flat, feel free to walk in, but with an appointment you won’t have to wait.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge until the day before. Just reply to your confirmation email or give us a call.

Do I have to wait while you work?

No. You drop off the bike, we agree on the price, and you pick it up once we’ve texted you.

Prices and payment

What if the repair costs more than quoted?

We call you before doing anything that goes beyond the agreed fixed price. Without your okay, we don’t continue.

How can I pay?

Debit card, credit card or cash. For company bikes, we can issue an invoice.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. We install parts you bring at our regular labor rates, but we can’t offer a warranty on the part itself.

E-bikes and cargo bikes

Do you repair all e-bike brands?

The mechanics, yes. To read out and update the motor system, we need the manufacturer’s diagnostic software. For less common systems, ask us first.

Do I need to bring the battery?

Yes, fully charged please. We can’t test the motor without it.

Will my cargo bike fit through the gate?

The courtyard entrance is 1.20 m (about 4 ft) wide. All common two- and three-wheel cargo bikes fit through.
