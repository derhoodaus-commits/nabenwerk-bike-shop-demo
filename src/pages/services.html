E-bike service

E-bikes are heavier and faster, so brakes, tires and chains wear out sooner. We check the mechanics more thoroughly and read out the motor’s error log.

Book e-bike service
  • Full mechanical tune-up
  • Error log read-out with report
  • Firmware update, if the manufacturer offers one
  • Check battery contacts and connectors
  • Brakes set up for the extra weight
