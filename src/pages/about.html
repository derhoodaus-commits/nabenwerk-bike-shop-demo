About

A small shop with firm rules

Nabenwerk is a three-stand workshop in a Neukölln courtyard. We don’t sell bikes. We keep them running.

Four rules we stick to

  1. Price first, then the work

    You get a fixed price before we pick up a wrench. If we find more along the way, we call you.

  2. Replace only what’s worn

    We measure instead of guessing. Parts that still have life in them stay on the bike. Want the old parts back? Just ask.

  3. Repair before replace

    A bent rim gets trued, a cable gets lubed, a hub gets new bearings. Buying new is the last resort.

  4. A report for every bike

    After every tune-up, you get an email listing what we did, what’s due soon, and what can wait.
