A €30 chain saves an €80 cassette

A stretched chain grinds down the cassette and chainrings. Replace it in time and you won’t need a whole new drivetrain.

That’s why we measure chain wear at every visit and tell you how much longer it will last. Free, even if you only came in for a flat.
